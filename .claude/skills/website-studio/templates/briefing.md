# Business Briefing

Mirrors the reusable Drive template:
docs.google.com/document/d/1uEQMykt9Nne2jWWxtu7jpnxCHec5ra4rQyr8x_y6H3g

**How to use this:** fill every field you can yourself before asking the
client anything - from their current site, Google Maps, Instagram, and
whatever is in the Drive folder. Mark each field with its source. Then ask
only about fields marked `ASK`, and only where the answer would change the
website.

Write the completed version back into the project's Drive briefing doc.

---

## 01 - Business
- What does the business do?
- Where? *(full address; the neighbourhood matters for local search and copy)*
- Main services/products?
- Prices?
- What does the business most want to grow? `ASK` *(this decides the whole site)*

## 02 - Customer
- Who are the main customers?
- Why do they come?
- What problem are they solving?
- Why do they choose this business over the alternative? `ASK`

## 03 - Story
- Who is behind the business?
- How did it start?
- Why do they care about this?
- Values?

## 04 - Proof
- Google Maps listing *(rating, review count, recurring themes in reviews)*
- Reviews worth quoting *(verbatim, with the reviewer's name as shown)*
- Awards
- Qualifications and certifications
- Notable customers or results

## 05 - Brand
- Instagram *(also: what do their best-performing posts look like?)*
- Current website
- Brands/websites they like
- What should the brand feel like?

## 06 - Website
- What should people do when they arrive? `ASK`
- WhatsApp / book / buy / visit / enquire? *(pick the one primary action)*
- Anything they definitely want on the site?
- **Language: one or two?** `ASK` *(pt-PT default; decides the markup)*

## 07 - Assets
- Logo *(vector? what formats exist?)*
- Photos *(how many usable, of what, shot how - see the photo audit)*
- Video
- Brand guidelines
- Other

## 08 - Access / links
- Website + who controls the domain
- Google Maps
- Instagram
- Booking system, if any
- WhatsApp number `ASK if missing` *(the site cannot ship without it)*
- Other relevant links
